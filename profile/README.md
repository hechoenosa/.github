## The 5 rules

1. **Do it scared.** You don't wait to feel ready.
2. **The 10-minute rule.** Stuck for 10 minutes? Ask, with a screenshot, what you tried and what you expected.
3. **No AI until Week 4.** Learn to read code first.
4. **Explain every line.** AI is allowed. Code nobody on your team can explain isn't.
5. **Stay private.** First names or handles only. No faces, phone numbers, addresses or passwords.
